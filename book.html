Check your dates

Pick your dates and you will see every line of the price before anything is charged, including both taxes. Nothing is held until you go through to payment.

Your details

Payment is handled by Stripe. Card details never touch this site. Your dates are held for 20 minutes while you pay.

Booking direct

No platform service fee. You are booking with the owner, and the email goes to a person rather than a queue.

A $500 security deposit is authorised against your card and released after checkout. It is held, not charged, and ordinary wear is not deducted from it.

Summer weeks run Saturday to Saturday and ski weeks Friday to Friday. Minimums vary by season — if your dates do not work the box above will say so and why.

Rather just ask?

Email stay@wirewalk.com. If you are trying to work out whether the house suits a particular group, that is usually a faster conversation than a form.
