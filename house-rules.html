House rules

Short, and mostly about things that are genuinely different in a rural house.

The basics

Check in from 4:00 pm, check out by 10:00 am. Ten people maximum, and that is the number the septic system and the bed count are built around rather than an arbitrary limit. No parties and no events — it is a house on a quiet road with neighbours who live here all year.

No smoking anywhere indoors. Pets are welcome with the pet fee; tell me in advance, and dogs are not left alone in the house.

The septic system

The house is on septic, not mains drainage. Nothing goes down the lavatory except what came out of you and ordinary toilet paper. No wipes, even the ones labelled flushable, which are the single most common cause of a rental holiday ending in a plumber. This is the one rule where ignoring it costs you the security deposit and costs me a weekend.

The wood stoves

Both work and you are welcome to use them. Wood is supplied. The doors stay shut when a fire is lit, ash goes in the metal bucket and not the bin, and the fire is out before you go to bed or leave the house. If you have never run a wood stove, message me and I will talk you through it — it is genuinely easy and much better than guessing.

Rubbish, and bears

There are black bears here. They are not a danger to you and they are extremely interested in your rubbish. Bin bags do not live on the deck overnight, ever. Everything goes in the bear-proof bin, and food does not stay out on the deck after dark. A bear that finds food at a house comes back to that house for years, which is a problem for whoever rents it next and eventually for the bear.

Winter

The drive is plowed. You still want proper winter tyres — summer tyres on this road in February are not a risk worth taking whatever the car. Shovel and roof rake are in the shed. If a storm is forecast for your arrival, message me and I will tell you what the road is actually doing.

If something breaks

Tell me. Genuinely — things break in houses, and I would far rather hear it from you on the Tuesday than find it on the Sunday. Nothing that is an ordinary accident comes out of the deposit. The deposit exists for damage that is not, and for the septic.

The lake

There is no lifeguard and there are no bathrooms at the beach. If small children are swimming, an adult is the lifeguard. Life jackets for the canoe are in the shed and children wear them.
