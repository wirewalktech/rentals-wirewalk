Rates are per night for the whole house, up to ten guests. A week is seven nights; the weekly rate is a 15% reduction on the nightly total, and stays of 28 nights or more take 35% off. Friday and Saturday nights carry a 25% uplift — the weekend is the part that is scarce.

The two taxes are collected on behalf of New York State and Warren County and passed straight on. They are not mine. New York began applying state sales tax to short-term rental occupancy in March 2025, so if you are comparing against an older listing somewhere else, check whether their figure includes it.

The deposit is held, not charged. It is authorised against your card and released after checkout. It is not taken as payment, it is never netted against the balance, and ordinary wear is not deducted from it. It exists for real damage and for the septic system.

Changeover days

Summer weeks run Saturday to Saturday. Ski weeks run Friday to Friday, which is the local convention and lets you get a full day on the mountain on the Saturday. Outside those seasons any day works.

Minimum stays

Two nights most of the year. Three at MLK weekend, four at Presidents week, five over Christmas and New Year, and summer is a full week. If your dates cross more than one season, the longer minimum applies — the booking page tells you before you get as far as paying.
