A house with room for everyone, twenty minutes from the mountain

Five bedrooms on twelve private acres in the southern Adirondacks, with rights to Antler Lake at the bottom of the road. Two wood stoves, a full kitchen, and enough space that twelve people are not living on top of each other.

It is a Cape Cod, and it has been lived in rather than staged. The kitchen counters are local garnet stone, quarried a few miles up the road at Barton Mines. Both wood stoves work and get used. The house comes furnished, so you arrive with food and clothes and nothing else.

What people tend to like most is the quiet. Twelve acres means the nearest neighbour is not in the window, and the only thing you hear at night is the wind. That is either exactly what you came for or it is not, and it is worth knowing which before you book.

Gore Mountain is twenty minutes away and the North Creek Ski Bowl, which is the better base if half your group is learning, is about ten. In summer the lake is a two-minute walk and the Hudson is close enough to raft in the morning and be back for lunch.

Booking direct

There is no platform booking fee here, which is most of the reason this site exists. On the big sites a guest typically pays a service fee on top of the nightly rate; booking here, that money stays in your pocket rather than going to a intermediary.

You are also emailing the person who owns the house. If the well pump fails or you cannot find the snow shovel, there is no support queue in between.

New York State and Warren County sales and occupancy taxes are added at checkout and shown as separate lines. They are collected on behalf of the state and the county and passed straight on — they are not ours.

Minimum stay depends on the season — two nights most of the year, longer over Christmas and the holiday ski weeks. The booking page works it out for your dates and tells you before you pay.

Winter driving, honestly. The last stretch of road is plowed but it is the Adirondacks in February. A front-wheel-drive car with proper winter tyres is usually fine; summer tyres are not, whatever the car. If a storm is forecast for your arrival day, message me and I will tell you what the road is actually doing rather than what the forecast says.
