The house

A five-bedroom Cape Cod, furnished and lived in rather than staged. Two wood stoves, a full kitchen, and twelve acres of quiet around it.

It sleeps ten across five bedrooms, which means nobody is on a sofa bed unless they want to be. Two bathrooms for ten people is the honest constraint of the house, and worth knowing before a ski morning when everyone wants to be out by half past eight.

The kitchen counters are garnet stone, quarried a few miles up the road at Barton Mines. It is the sort of detail you notice on the second day. Both wood stoves work, there is wood, and the second one is in the room you will actually sit in.

What is here

Full kitchen with oven, hob, fridge-freezer, microwave, coffee maker, and enough crockery and pans for ten. Washer and dryer. Wi-fi. Two wood stoves with wood supplied. Barbecue on the deck. Off-street parking for several cars.

Bed linen and towels are provided, which is not universal around here — a good number of Adirondack rentals ask you to bring your own, so it is worth saying.

What is not

No hot tub. No air conditioning, which has never once been a problem at this altitude. No dishwasher. Mobile signal is patchy depending on your carrier, and there is wi-fi for exactly that reason.

It is a proper Adirondack house rather than a hotel. If something is missing that you need, message me before you come and I will usually sort it.
